30-Day Spoken English Course

Follow one lesson every day.

DAY 1

Self Introduction

Learn how to introduce yourself.

Example:

My name is Rahul. I am from Bihar. I am a student. I am learning English.

DAY 2

Greetings

Learn common greetings.

Hello! How are you?

I am fine, thank you.

Nice to meet you.

DAY 3

At Home

Talk about things you do at home.

I am at home.

I am having breakfast.

I am cleaning my room.

DAY 4

Daily Routine

Talk about your everyday routine.

I wake up at six o'clock.

I brush my teeth.

I go to work.

DAY 5

Shopping

Learn useful shopping sentences.

How much is this?

I want to buy this shirt.

Do you have a smaller size?

DAY 6

Travel

Useful English for travelling.

Where is the railway station?

How much is the ticket?

When does the train arrive?

DAY 7

At School

Talk about school and classroom.

Open your book.

Please sit down.

I have a question.

DAY 8

Friends

Talk about your friends.

He is my best friend.

We study together.

We play cricket together.

DAY 9

Food

Talk about food and eating.

I am hungry.

I like rice.

What would you like to eat?

DAY 10

Weather

Talk about the weather.

It is very hot today.

It is raining.

The weather is pleasant.

DAY 11

Work

Talk about your work.

I go to work every day.

I am busy today.

I have a lot of work.

DAY 12

Future Plans

Talk about your plans.

I will call you tomorrow.

I will go there next week.

I am going to learn English.

DAY 13

Past Activities

I went to the market yesterday.

I watched a movie.

I met my friend.

DAY 14

Making Questions

What is your name?

Where do you live?

What do you do?

DAY 15

Halfway Challenge

Speak about yourself for one minute without stopping.

🗣️ Today's Speaking Practice

Read the sentence. Listen. Then speak aloud.

Topic: My Daily Routine

I wake up early in the morning.
मैं सुबह जल्दी उठता हूँ।
I brush my teeth and take a bath.
मैं अपने दाँत साफ करता हूँ और स्नान करता हूँ।
I have breakfast with my family.
मैं अपने परिवार के साथ नाश्ता करता हूँ।
Then I go to work.
फिर मैं काम पर जाता हूँ।
I come home in the evening.
मैं शाम को घर आता हूँ।
Speaking Tip:

Do not try to speak very fast. Speak slowly and clearly. Repeat each sentence 3 times.

Practice:
First time — Read
Second time — Look and speak
Third time — Speak without looking
